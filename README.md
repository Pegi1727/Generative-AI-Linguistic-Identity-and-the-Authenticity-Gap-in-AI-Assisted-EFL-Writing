## 📖 Study Overview

This mixed-methods study investigates the linguistic, psychological, and identity-related consequences of generative artificial intelligence in English as a Foreign Language (EFL) writing.

The study focuses on advanced EFL learners who use generative AI, particularly GPT-4o, to revise and improve their written production. Although AI-assisted revision may improve linguistic accuracy, complexity, and fluency, it may also create a discrepancy between the learner’s original linguistic identity and the polished version produced with AI assistance.

This discrepancy is conceptualized as the Authenticity Gap.

The study examines how AI-assisted writing affects:
- Linguistic complexity and Lexical diversity
- Grammatical accuracy and overall text quality
- Perceived authenticity and Linguistic identity
- Identity compression and Learners’ attitudes toward AI-assisted writing

The dataset includes quantitative linguistic and psychometric data, as well as qualitative interview data from 60 EFL learners.

---

## 🎯 Research Objectives

The study addresses the following objectives:
1. To compare learners’ original and AI-assisted written texts.
2. To examine changes in linguistic complexity, accuracy, and lexical diversity.
3. To investigate learners’ perceptions of authenticity when using generative AI.
4. To explore the relationship between AI-assisted writing and linguistic identity.
5. To examine whether AI-assisted revision contributes to identity compression.
6. To integrate quantitative findings with qualitative interview evidence.
7. To develop an empirically informed account of the Authenticity Gap in AI-assisted EFL writing.

---

## 🔬 Research Design

The project uses a mixed-methods research design consisting of:

### 📊 Quantitative Component
- Within-participant comparisons of writing performance before and after AI assistance.
- Two-way analysis of variance (ANOVA) and paired-samples comparisons.
- Pearson correlation analyses and Linear mixed-effects modeling (LMEM).
- Psychometric analysis of the Authenticity Gap Scale.

### 🗣️ Qualitative Component
- Semi-structured interviews and thematic analysis.
- Interview coding from a subsample of 15 participants*.
- Integration of qualitative evidence with quantitative results.

## 🧠 Conceptual Framework: The Authenticity Gap

The central concept of this project is the *Authenticity Gap, referring to the perceived distance between:
1. A learner’s original written production and linguistic identity.
2. The linguistically improved version produced with the assistance of generative AI.

While AI-assisted writing produces more sophisticated texts, it may lead to Identity Compression, where the learner's unique voice is replaced by a standardized AI persona.
